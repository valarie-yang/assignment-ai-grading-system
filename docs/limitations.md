# Limitations and next steps

## Implemented or verified

- Offline assessment workflow and local persistence.
- Rubric and submission domain flows.
- Structured local AI bridge path.
- Teacher review before final result.
- Local verification and secret-boundary checks.

## Proposed or gated

- Production authentication, authorization, database, and object storage.
- Immutable model-version and prompt-version capture.
- Asynchronous worker, retries, monitoring, and incident recovery.
- Golden-set evaluation with adjudicated teacher labels.
- Institutional retention and deletion policy.
- Real external-provider quality measurement.

## Not claimed

- Autonomous grading.
- Production deployment.
- Institutional compliance certification.
- Model fairness or accuracy without a documented evaluation.
- Real student outcomes or time savings.
